Read before you react.

Review messages and links with on-device checks. Extract text and QR codes from selected images, supported iPhone/iPad live scanning, or a Mac camera still image.

Recognition can make mistakes. No warning found means unverified, not safe. Images do not prove authenticity.

02 / SAFARI

Rules you can understand.

Choose optional CERT Polska domain-list downloads and manage your own blocks and exceptions. Review source age and Safari’s reported extension state separately.

Applies to matching Safari requests when rules are loaded and enabled. The source focuses on threats targeting Polish users and is not complete global coverage.

Discover supported services advertised on a network you are authorized to manage. See available addresses and organize observations with room labels you assign.

A partial service view, not a complete device inventory or people tracker. Room labels do not measure physical locations.

Before you get started

A few things to know.

Can Vira tell me whether something is real or fake?

Vira can recognize text and QR codes, check for supported warning patterns, and compare destinations with an optional locally cached source. It cannot conclusively authenticate a person, sender, product, document, photo, or video. Verify important requests through a trusted channel you already know.

Does Safari protection update while the app is closed?

Background timing is not guaranteed. After you opt in, Vira attempts source updates about every five minutes while active and when you return if due. Safari may keep its last loaded rules while Vira is closed, including entries later removed from the source. Reopen Vira to refresh, or disable its extension to stop blocking.

Will the network map show where someone is?

No. It shows supported advertised services and available addresses. Names may be spoofed, some devices advertise nothing, and multiple services can belong to one device. Room labels are entered by you; the app does not identify people or measure a device’s physical location.

Where can I download Vira?

Vira is still in development. There is no public App Store download announced here. Camera behavior, native Safari enforcement, permissions, and supported-device workflows require release testing before broader availability.
