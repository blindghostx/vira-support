Privacy policy

What stays local. What connects.

Policy version: October 31, 2026 · iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Checks stay on your device

Vira reads text and QR codes from camera input or images you choose and checks text and links on your device. It does not upload this content for analysis, retain camera recordings, or save a separate copy of your selected image. If a selected image is stored in iCloud, Apple’s photo or file picker may download it before Vira reads it.

Session information

Submitted text, findings, discovered service names and addresses, and room labels are held in app memory. Use Clear submitted content & network results to remove them from the current session. Vira does not send this information to a server. Network discovery sends local service-discovery requests on the network you choose to inspect.

Saved Safari settings

Downloaded threat domains, your custom blocks and exceptions, and rule settings are stored locally for Vira and its Safari extension. The current configuration and one previous configuration are retained for rollback. These files are excluded from backups.

Removing a source or domain changes the active rules; the previous configuration may still contain it. Clearing a check session does not erase saved protection settings. Vira does not record Safari browsing history or blocked-page activity.

Use Reset saved Safari configuration in Vira’s Privacy screen to remove downloaded sources, custom domains, and current and previous configurations. Vira then asks Safari to load empty rules. If Safari cannot apply the change, disable the extension in Safari settings to stop its previously loaded rules.

Optional threat-list downloads

If you enable the CERT Polska source, Vira downloads its public domain list directly over HTTPS. CERT Polska receives normal connection metadata, including your IP address and Vira’s User-Agent. Vira does not send it your submitted content or browsing activity. The provider’s own privacy practices apply to connection metadata it receives.

After you opt in, Vira attempts refreshes about every five minutes while the app is active and when you return. Background timing is not guaranteed. Remove the source to stop its updates. Read more about the CERT Polska Warning List.

Permissions and controls

Camera and local network access are requested for features that need them. You can decline access or change permissions in system settings. You control Safari extension enablement in Safari settings. You can edit active blocks and exceptions in Vira, pause configured rules, or disable the extension in Safari.

This version has no Vira account, cloud analysis service, advertising, or analytics SDK.

Website hosting

When you visit Vira’s website on GitHub Pages, GitHub records and retains your IP address for security, including when you are not signed in to GitHub. Vira adds no analytics, advertising, forms, or external scripts to these pages. Website hosting is separate from the app’s on-device checks.

External links and support

Recovery and support links open only when you choose them. Those websites and your email provider then apply their own privacy practices. Selecting the support email link opens your email app; it does not send a message automatically.

Our support mailbox uses Gmail. If you email us, we and Gmail receive the sender details, message, and attachments you choose to send. Correspondence is kept in the support mailbox to handle your request. Contact us about access or deletion of correspondence we hold. Copies in your email account or provider systems remain subject to their controls. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary private content.

Questions and changes

Contact viratechnologieLLC@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. We will update this policy when Vira’s data practices change.
